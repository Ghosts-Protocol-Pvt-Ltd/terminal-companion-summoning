# Art credits

The ASCII figures in `templates/forms/` were not drawn by this project. They are
the work of ASCII artists, most of them made years before this repo existed, and
they are the reason the gallery is worth browsing at all.

## Where the art came from

The figures were curated from [asciiart.eu](https://www.asciiart.eu/), which
collects and mirrors ASCII art from across the web. The curation notes, including
which source page each batch came from, are in [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md).

Every artist signature that was present in the original art has been kept inside
the art itself. If you open a form file and see initials tucked into a corner,
that is the artist signing their work, and it stays there. Nothing was cropped or
cleaned off.

project, which is not the same as their being unowned.

## What the licence covers

The MIT licence on this repository covers the code: `summon.sh`, the templates,
and the documentation. It does not, and cannot, cover the ASCII art, because the
art was never this project's to relicense. Each figure remains the property of
whoever drew it.

The art is included here in the same spirit it was published in: freely readable,
freely shared, credited to its maker.

## If you are an artist here and want your work removed

Open an issue, or email the address on the repository owner's GitHub profile, and
say which figures are yours. They will be removed, no questions asked and no
argument made. You do not need to prove anything.

That offer is not a formality. The gallery is nice to have. It is not worth
keeping a single piece of art that its maker would rather was not here.
